1. Purpose

This Agreement governs the terms under which the Author publishes original written works ("Content") on the Auror.my platform and receives a share of revenue generated from that Content through the Platform's monetization mechanisms: Coin Purchases and Subscriptions.

2. Definitions

TermMeaning
PlatformThe Auror.my web and desktop application and all associated services
ContentAny story, chapter, or written work published by the Author on the Platform
CoinsThe Platform's virtual currency purchased by Readers to unlock premium chapters
Coin RevenueGross revenue collected by the Platform from Reader coin top-up purchases
Subscription RevenueGross revenue collected from Reader subscription fees
Author EarningsThe Author's share of Coin Revenue and/or Subscription Revenue as defined in Section 4
PayoutA disbursement of Author Earnings to the Author's registered bank account or e-wallet
ReaderAny registered user of the Platform who reads and/or purchases Content
Premium ChapterA chapter designated by the Author as coin-locked, requiring Readers to spend Coins to access

3. Author Obligations

3.1 Original Content

The Author warrants that all Content published on the Platform:

  • Is the Author's own original work.
  • Does not infringe any third-party copyright, trademark, or intellectual property right.
  • Does not contain plagiarised material.
  • Does not violate Malaysian law, including but not limited to the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998, the Penal Code, and applicable religious sensitivity guidelines.

3.2 Content Standards

The Author agrees not to publish Content that:

  • Contains sexually explicit material involving minors.
  • Promotes violence, terrorism, or racial hatred.
  • Violates Malaysian Islamic guidelines where applicable.
  • Is spam, machine-generated, or AI-generated without meaningful original authorship.

3.3 Account Responsibility

The Author is responsible for maintaining the security of their account credentials and for all activity conducted under their account.

3.4 Tax Compliance

The Author is solely responsible for declaring and paying any applicable Malaysian income tax on Author Earnings received. The Platform may issue a CP58 statement where required by law.

4. Revenue Split

4.1 Coin Revenue Split

When a Reader spends Coins to unlock a Premium Chapter authored by the Author:

Coin Purchase Split
Author 70%
Platform (Auror.my) 30%
Example: A Reader spends RM 10.00 worth of Coins to unlock a chapter. The Author receives RM 7.00; the Platform retains RM 3.00 to cover payment processing, infrastructure, fraud prevention, and operations.

4.2 Subscription Revenue Split

When a Reader holds an active subscription that grants access to the Author's Content:

Subscription Split
Author 60%
Platform (Auror.my) 40%
Example: A Reader pays RM 15.00/month. If the Author's Content accounts for 20% of that Reader's reading activity in the billing period, the Author's attributable revenue is RM 3.00, of which the Author receives RM 1.80 (60%).
Subscription Attribution: Subscription Revenue is distributed among eligible Authors based on a pro-rata formula determined by the Platform, taking into account chapters read, Reader engagement, and Content volume during the subscription period. The current attribution methodology is published in the Author Dashboard.

4.3 Tips (Future Feature)

Tip revenue, when enabled, will be passed to the Author at 100% less applicable payment processing fees. This section will be updated when the tip feature is activated.

4.4 No Guaranteed Earnings

The Platform does not guarantee any minimum level of Author Earnings. Earnings depend entirely on Reader activity and Content performance.

5. Payouts

5.1 Minimum Payout Threshold

Payouts are processed once the Author's accumulated Author Earnings reach a minimum balance of RM 50.00 (or such other amount as the Platform may set from time to time with 30 days' notice).

5.2 Payout Schedule

Payouts are processed on a monthly basis, typically within the first 14 days of the following calendar month, subject to:

  • The Author having a verified payout method on file.
  • The Author's account being in good standing (not suspended or under investigation).
  • Admin approval of the payout request.

5.3 Payout Methods

Supported payout methods include: FPX bank transfer, DuitNow transfer, Touch 'n Go (TnG) e-wallet, and Boost e-wallet. The Platform reserves the right to add or remove payout methods with 30 days' notice.

5.4 Payout Fees

The Platform does not charge a payout processing fee. However, the Author's bank or e-wallet provider may impose their own transfer fees, which are the Author's responsibility.

5.5 Payout History

The Author may download payout statements from the Author Dashboard at any time.

5.6 Withheld Payouts

The Platform may withhold a Payout if the Author's account is under investigation for fraud or policy violations, a valid legal order has been received, or the Author's payout details are incomplete or unverified.

8. Term and Termination

8.1 Term

This Agreement commences on the date the Author creates an account on the Platform and continues until terminated by either party.

8.2 Termination by Author

The Author may terminate this Agreement at any time by closing their account via Platform settings or by written notice to legal@auror.my. Accrued Author Earnings above the minimum payout threshold will be disbursed within 60 days of account closure.

Upon termination for cause involving fraudulent activity, the Platform may forfeit accrued Author Earnings.
