## 4. REVENUE SPLIT

### 4.1 Coin Revenue Split

When a Reader spends Coins to unlock a Premium Chapter authored by the Author:

| Party | Share |
|-------|-------|
| **Author** | **70%** of the Coin Revenue attributable to that chapter |
| **Platform** | **30%** of the Coin Revenue attributable to that chapter |

> **Example:** A Reader spends RM 10.00 worth of Coins to unlock a chapter. The Author receives RM 7.00; the Platform retains RM 3.00.

The Platform's 70% share covers payment processing fees, platform infrastructure, hosting, fraud prevention, customer support, and operational costs.

### 4.2 Subscription Revenue Split

When a Reader holds an active subscription that grants access to the Author's Content:

| Party | Share |
|-------|-------|
| **Author** | **60%** of the Subscription Revenue attributable to the Author's Content |
| **Platform** | **40%** of the Subscription Revenue attributable to the Author's Content |

> **Note on Attribution:** Subscription Revenue is distributed among eligible Authors based on a pro-rata formula determined by the Platform, taking into account factors such as chapters read, Reader engagement, and Content volume during the subscription period. The Platform will publish the current attribution methodology in the Author Dashboard.

> **Example:** A Reader pays RM 15.00/month for a subscription. If the Author's Content accounts for 20% of that Reader's reading activity in the billing period, the Author's attributable revenue is RM 3.00, of which the Author receives RM 1.80 (60%) and the Platform retains RM 1.20 (40%).

### 4.3 Tips (Future)
Tip revenue, when enabled, will be passed to the Author at **100% less applicable payment processing fees**. This section will be updated when the tip feature is activated.

### 4.4 No Guaranteed Earnings
The Platform does not guarantee any minimum level of Author Earnings. Earnings depend entirely on Reader activity and Content performance.

## 5. PAYOUTS

### 5.1 Minimum Payout Threshold
Payouts are processed once the Author's accumulated Author Earnings reach a minimum balance of **RM 50.00** (or such other amount as the Platform may set from time to time with 30 days' notice).

### 5.2 Payout Schedule
Payouts are processed on a **monthly** basis, typically within the first 14 days of the following calendar month, subject to:
- The Author having a verified payout method on file.
- The Author's account being in good standing (not suspended or under investigation).
- Admin approval of the payout request.

### 5.3 Payout Methods
Supported payout methods include:
- FPX bank transfer
- DuitNow transfer
- Touch 'n Go (TnG) e-wallet
- Boost e-wallet

The Platform reserves the right to add or remove payout methods with 30 days' notice.

### 5.4 Payout Fees
The Platform does not charge a payout processing fee. However, the Author's bank or e-wallet provider may impose their own transfer fees, which are the Author's responsibility.

### 5.5 Payout History
The Author may download payout statements from the Author Dashboard at any time.

### 5.6 Withheld Payouts
The Platform may withhold a Payout if:
- The Author's account is under investigation for fraud, abuse, or policy violations.
- The Platform has received a valid legal order to withhold funds.
- The Author's payout details are incomplete or unverified.
